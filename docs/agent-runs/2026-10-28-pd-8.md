## Rulings

- Ruling: follow design.md 7.2 (hero card, two stat tiles) rather than the ticket's "gauge with 30% and 10% markers" - design.md is the UI source of truth and 6.6 says "Never a ring or gauge"; the stat tile bar carries the 30% tick - cost if wrong: swap one component for a gauge later.
- Ruling: "top three Next actions" = the hero (the earliest pay-by card, its minimum and statement steps merged into one payment) plus the next two other plan steps under "Coming up" - that is how design.md 7.2 lays out the plan on Home - cost if wrong: re-slice the model in `adapter.ts`.
- Ruling: the hero is the item with the earliest pay-by date, not `plan.items[0]` - design.md 6.8 defines the primary step as the earliest unpaid by pay-by date; after the Axis minimum is recorded the planner's first item is HDFC (4 Nov) while Axis (22 Oct) is still due - cost if wrong: one sort in `buildHero`.
- Ruling: "steps done" = number of recorded payments, total = remaining plan items + recorded payments - there is no per-step paid state until PD-9 (shared step state); it is an approximation that is exact for one payment per step - cost if wrong: the hero progress bar is off by one after a payment that clears two steps. PD-9 should replace it.
- Ruling: no "Create account" / "Sign in" on Welcome and no "Manage" on Home - none has a destination in this wave (auth and a Cards screen are other tickets); a dead control on the judges' path is worse than a missing one - cost if wrong: add two buttons later.
- Ruling: the Cards tab in the floating nav and the Reminders / account buttons in the header are no-ops for now (no routes exist). The Cards tab keeps `FloatingNav`'s fixed four tabs.
- Ruling: a card with a limit of 0 or missing is dropped from the tiles and from utilization (engine does this) and a note says so ("X has no credit limit on file, so it is left out of your utilization.") - the sentence is mine because design.md section 9 has no copy for it - cost if wrong: copy edit.
- Ruling: a null `dueDay` (SBI, paid in full) is passed to the engine as the statement day. The engine only reads `dueDay` when there is something left to pay on the statement, so SBI has no due date and the tile says "nothing due".
- Ruling: the headline when nothing is left to pay is "Aarav, you're all set for now." and the body is the planner's own note - no design.md copy exists.
